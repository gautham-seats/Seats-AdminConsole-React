The mix — three versions

All three carry everything you asked for: aurora lights behind every bar, the cyan line under the bar from Momentum, the shadow from Slab, a round icon, and bold text. They differ only in how deep the blue goes, how strong the lift is, and how the cyan line is drawn. Each is shown as a card header, a table header and a dialog header, because the one you pick is used on all three, on every page.

A · Balanced

recommended

The nav bar's own left-to-right blue, two drifting aurora layers and a slow sweep. The cyan line starts bright at the left and fades out at 88%. Shadow is present but gentle. Title 16px bold.
