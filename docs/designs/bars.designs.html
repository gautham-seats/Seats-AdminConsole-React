Admin bars — five variants

Every variant is built from the same tokens the app already ships: #135d93 / #1566a2 / #145f9c, the same font stack as the nav bar and the Scheduler (both declare no custom font), and the same shared button gradient. Each variant is shown three times — as a card header, a table header and a dialog header — because whichever you pick is applied to all three across every page. Titles are larger than today in all five.

1 · Aurora

closest to today

The nav bar's own recipe, turned up: drifting aurora clouds, a slow light sweep every nine seconds and a cyan hairline along the bottom. Title 15.5px. Familiar, alive, never loud.

2 · Glass rail

A bright cyan rail down the left edge marks every bar, over deep translucent blue with a slow blurred glow behind it. The icon becomes a circle. Calm, modern, easy to scan down a long page of cards.

4 · Slab

no motion

A solid, slightly deeper blue with a gloss across the top half and a shadow that lifts the bar off the card. No animation at all, so a page full of cards stays completely still — and it is the lightest on your laptop.
