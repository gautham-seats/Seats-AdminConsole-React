Access Profile — live preview, three variations

Today the Live Preview reads only Site Access: it is handed groups and selectedPermissions and nothing else, so ticking Event, Case or Workflow visibility cannot move it. These three variations each answer the same question — how do the other three areas get into the preview — and keep the panel you already like: the blue glass, the aurora drift, the 18s sheen, the updating dot, the typing effect.

These are live. In each variation, switch tabs and toggle rows — the preview beside it reacts exactly as it would in the app: counters count, bars slide, the dot turns to “updating…”, the sentence retypes itself. Each variation keeps its own state, so you can leave them in different shapes and compare.

Variation A

Four Lenses — one panel, four faces

The preview follows the tab you are editing. On Site Access it is exactly what you have today. On the other three it becomes a full-size briefing for that area: its own three counters, its own sentence, its own “what stays hidden”. Each area gets the whole panel rather than a corner of it.

Variation B

The Ledger — everything at once

The Site Access briefing stays exactly where it is. Three compact rows join underneath and are always on screen: icon, count, a mini bar. The tab you are editing expands to name what is selected; the others stay one line. You can see in one glance that a profile has wide page access but almost no case visibility.

Variation C

Day in the Life — what it feels like to use

Counts stop being the point. The panel narrates what the person holding this profile actually experiences, one sentence per area, and every sentence is a link into the tab that owns it. Whichever sentence just changed flashes and retypes. Four counters sit on top for the audit trail.
