Room details — five layouts

Five ways to rebuild /resources/rooms/[id]. Today the page holds four fields (Building, Room code, Room name, Capacity) in one small card on an empty workspace — Gautham rated it 1/10 on 23 Sep 2026. Each layout below says how to arrange the page and what to put on it, because the real problem is that there is not enough on the page to arrange.

What the page looks like now

A 240px identity box beside a card of four fields, sitting in the top-left of a full-height workspace. Nothing else. No devices, no history, no usage — so the page cannot help you do anything except rename a room.

Layout 4

Split editor with live preview

Layout 5

Drawer over the rooms grid

Do not navigate at all. Clicking a room slides a panel over the grid, so you can edit ten rooms without losing your filters, your page or your scroll position. A link out to the full page stays for the heavier tabs. Click any row below.
